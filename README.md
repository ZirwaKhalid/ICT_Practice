# What ICT Means to Me
ICT means Information and Communication Technology. To me, ICT is an important part of modern education, communication, engineering, and problem-solving.
As a first-year Mechatronics Engineering student at Air University, I see ICT as a connection between technology, programming, electronics, automation, and intelligent systems. I want to develop my ICT skills and use them to solve practical engineering problems and contribute to innovative projects in the future.
